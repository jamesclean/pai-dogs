# Tags and collars: sourcing notes (Nov 2026)

These came from search listings that couldn't all be opened, so treat prices
and MOQs as approximate. Get samples and a written quote before ordering.

## Tags

**Spec to send:** 304 or 316 stainless, slide-on (two slots), slot ≥ 26 × 4 mm
so it fits coated 25 mm webbing (which is ~3 mm thick). QR **fiber-laser
engraved / black-annealed**, not UV-printed (fades) and not on black-coated
tags (won't scan). QR ≥ 18 mm square, with the 5-character code engraved
underneath. Use the CSV from Admin → Tags → batch → "Download CSV". Each row
is one tag with its own code.

**Ask for a sample of 5 different codes first** and scan them with an old
Android phone in shade.

- **Alibaba, slide-on with engraved QR:** Zhongshan Gushi lists "Customize Blank Stainless Steel … Slide on Pet Engraved QR Code" at ~$0.19–0.39, MOQ 100. Another listing explicitly offers "Different QR Code Numbers for Each Tag", MOQ 100 (search Alibaba "stainless steel qr plate"). Confirm per-piece variable data from a CSV.
- **Thailand:** no seller confirmed for bulk unique-QR slide-on tags. Options to ask: Hobby QR (Shopee: hobbyqr) and Mr.Mrs.Sniff (LINE shop @mr.mrs.sniff), which do QR pet tags, mostly round and hanging. A Chiang Mai fiber-laser engraving shop working on plain slide-on blanks is the local backup.
- **Western comparison:** Boomerang slide-on stainless, deep-engraved, ~$13 each. Its "Large" fits 1" collars and there's a double-thick version for waterproof collars.

## Collars

**Spec:** 25 mm (1") TPU/PVC-coated webbing ("biothane-type"): waterproof,
doesn't rot or smell, easy to clean. Use a metal buckle, with reflective
stripe if offered, in colour international orange (#FF4F00 / Pantone 1655 C
is close; send a swatch). No velcro or breakaway clips on street dogs.

- **Dongguan Petisland** (Alibaba): waterproof TPU collar with logo, ~$2.90, MOQ 100.
- **Baiqiang Rubber & Plastic, Dongguan** (made-in-china.com): TPU-coated collars in orange, custom logo, MOQ 1,000.
- **Coated webbing by the metre** (~$0.33/m, MOQ 500–1,000 m) if a local saddler or bag maker in Chiang Mai can make the collars.
- **Retail benchmark:** Gun Dog Supply 1" hunter-orange reflective Biothane, ~$17.
- Shopee/Lazada TH had no coated collars, only plain nylon at ฿69–119, which rots in rainy season.

## Fitting

- Two fingers under the collar. Recheck puppies and young dogs every few weeks: an outgrown collar becomes an embedded wound (a Chai Nat case was in the news).
- Ear-notch at sterilization as well, so a dog that loses its collar is still known to be done.
- A Zanzibar study found collars made owners more willing to bring dogs for rabies vaccination. The orange recognition idea has support.
